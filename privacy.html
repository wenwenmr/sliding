Privacy Policy

App: Sliding Block Puzzle
Developer: wenwgame
Effective date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use Sliding Block Puzzle (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how data is collected and used.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not require you to create an account or register. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not operate our own servers to store personal data about you.

2. Data Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party SDKs that may automatically collect certain technical and usage data to support functionality, advertising, and analytics.

a. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Device information (model, manufacturer, OS version, language, screen resolution)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, close events)

This data is used to serve relevant ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements. In regions where consent is required (e.g., EEA, UK, Switzerland), we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before personalized advertising is shown.

b. Firebase Analytics (Usage Analytics)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players interact with the game. Data collected may include:

  • App launch events, screen views, and session duration
  • Gameplay events (level started, level completed, hints used, failures)
  • Device information and app version

This helps us improve game balance, fix bugs, and enhance the overall player experience. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized where possible.

c. Firebase Crashlytics (Crash Reporting)

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information when the App encounters an error. This may include:

  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Device state at the time of crash (device model, OS version, available memory, app version)
  • Recent app activity leading to the crash

This data is used solely to identify and fix stability issues.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads or purchasing hint packs. All purchases are processed by Google Play Billing.

  • We do not collect or store your credit card details or payment information.
  • Google Play processes all payment data under its own Privacy Policy.
  • We receive limited transaction metadata (such as product ID, purchase timestamp, and whether a purchase was successful) solely to validate and deliver the purchased content within the App.

4. How We Use Data

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • To serve and measure advertisements (via AdMob)
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve gameplay (via Firebase Analytics)
  • To detect, investigate, and fix crashes and stability issues (via Crashlytics)
  • To validate and fulfill in-app purchases (via Google Play Billing)
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data collected by the App may be shared with the following third-party service providers:

  • Google AdMob – for ad delivery, measurement, and personalization (where permitted)
  • Google Firebase – for analytics and crash reporting
  • Google Play – for purchase processing and entitlement verification

These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from partners: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Firebase Privacy & Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • Analytics: You can limit analytics collection where your device settings permit.
  • Data Deletion: Since we do not maintain user accounts or store personal data on our servers, uninstalling the App removes all local game data. For any concerns, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Sliding Block Puzzle is not directed specifically to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Local game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App. Third-party service providers retain data according to their respective retention policies.

9. Security

All network communication between the App and third-party services is encrypted using HTTPS. We take reasonable measures to protect data in transit, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
